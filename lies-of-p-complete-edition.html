Lies of P: Complete Edition bundles the acclaimed base game with its new 'Overture' expansion, giving both newcomers and returning puppeteers a single package to jump back into Krat.

The Overture DLC expands the grim, fairy-tale-inspired narrative with new districts to explore, additional boss encounters, and refinements to the weapon-assembly system that longtime fans have been asking for.

The developers say the Complete Edition is built to be the definitive way to experience the story, with balancing changes carried over from over a year of post-launch feedback.
