Release

CD Projekt Red has announced its new expansion, "Phantom City," featuring a complete redesign of Night City's districts. The update brings reworked lighting, new architecture, and an entirely fresh set of side-quest lines.

The biggest change lands in the skill tree system — fully rebalanced so different playstyles (netrunner, solo, technician) feel more distinct and impactful than ever.

Release Timeline

Developers say "Phantom City" is arriving this winter. More details on pre-load and system requirements are expected to drop over the coming weeks.
